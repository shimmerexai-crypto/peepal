Our Story

A cafe that feels like a pause button.

Peepal Cafe & Kitchen is built around one simple idea – good food deserves a good atmosphere. Under twinkling lights and green corners, we serve everything from a quick cold coffee to a full family dinner.

Our kitchen mixes North Indian classics, Italian favourites, Chinese cravings and Marwari flavours, so everyone at the table finds something they love.
